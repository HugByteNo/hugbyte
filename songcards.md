# Songcards

> **Your songs have a history. Keep it.**

**[Explore Songcards](https://songcards.hugbyte.no/)**

Songcards is a **local-first Windows desktop app** for the details that tend to get lost somewhere between making a song and releasing it.

Prompts, lyrics, generation settings, credits, rights notes, identifiers, and release links belong in one searchable place — not in a dozen tabs and a spreadsheet that was supposed to be temporary.

## What it does

- Keeps a searchable library of songs and their creative history.
- Records optional Suno generation information alongside lyrics and production notes.
- Tracks credits, rights-review notes, release details, and useful links.
- Supports local backup and portable exports.

## Why local-first?

The working library stays on your own Windows computer. No Songcards account, no cloud library, and no subscription are required for everyday use.

Songcards **doesn't generate music** and doesn't connect to a private Suno account. It's where you keep the record of what you've made.
