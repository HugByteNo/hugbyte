# Tight@Email

> **Less inbox noise. More reasons to open the next email.**

**[Visit Tight@Email](https://tight.email/)**

Tight@Email is an evolving publishing and reader-experience project: small interest-based worlds, a more playful front door, and email that aims to be worth receiving.

The site and its experiences are being developed independently, with a deliberately lean setup.

## The directions in the workshop

- **FUTURE | AUTOMATED //** — practical AI, technology, and automation.
- **OVERDRIVEN //** — music, without genre gatekeeping.
- **Konstruert Kaos //** — society, systems, and the occasional sharp elbow. Norwegian-first.
- **Independent creators** — tools, freedom, and building on your own terms; identity still being shaped.

Behind the scenes: a static Astro frontend and a self-hosted Listmonk mailing-list backend. The idea is to keep the publishing machinery boring so the experience doesn't have to be.

**Status:** Active work in progress. This page describes the direction, not a promise that every world or feature is already launched.
